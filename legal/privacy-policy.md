# Privacy Policy — Grit

Last updated: August 2026

Grit ("we", "the app") is a strength-training planner and workout log.

## What we collect

- **Account information:** email address and optional display name when you sign up.
- **Workout data:** training plans, logged sets (weight, reps), session history, and profile stats.
- **Technical data:** authentication tokens required to keep you signed in.

## What we do not collect

- Location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data.
- Payment information (the app is free).

## How data is stored

Your data is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL) with row-level security. Only your account can access your data. Transmission uses HTTPS.

## Data deletion

Delete your account from **Profile → Delete account** in the app. This permanently removes your profile, plans, sessions, and logged sets.

## Third parties

- **Supabase** provides authentication and database hosting.

We do not sell your personal data.

## Contact

Use the support email on the Google Play store listing for privacy questions.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
